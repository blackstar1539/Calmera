# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: a dependency-free static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript site so it can load quickly, keep personal data on-device, and deploy directly to GitHub Pages.

## Users

Teen and Gen Z visitors who want a low-pressure private space for a brief daily emotional check-in, grounding exercise, or journal entry.

## Product Purpose

Calmera is a browser-based daily wellness companion. It helps visitors pause, name how they feel, breathe, write privately, and find reputable support resources. Success means the core actions feel safe, quick, understandable, and worth returning to.

## Positioning

Calmera combines several tiny self-care rituals in one private, no-account experience; journal and mood data remain in the visitor's browser rather than being sent to a service.

## Operating Context

The product is used in short sessions on phones or laptops, often when the visitor is distracted, stressed, tired, or emotionally overloaded. It should work without sign-up and make every primary action understandable at a glance.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Daily affirmations with a control to request another.
- A mood check-in with supportive, non-diagnostic feedback and local history.
- A guided 4-7-8 breathing cycle with clear timing and stop controls.
- A private journal stored in `localStorage`, with search, deletion, and text/JSON export.
- Concise wellness tips and links to reputable crisis/support resources.
- Static hosting on GitHub Pages; no backend, account system, analytics, or remote storage.
- The site is a self-care tool, not a replacement for professional care or emergency services.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Calmera.
- Voice: calm, direct, validating, and contemporary; never childish, patronizing, clinical, or overly cheerful.
- Personal entries stay on the visitor's device.

## Evidence on Hand

The supplied legacy HTML contains the existing feature set and product copy. No testimonials, clinical evidence, customer logos, or commercial claims were supplied and none should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Reduce the effort required to take one helpful next step.
- Treat privacy as part of the experience, not fine print.
- Validate emotions without diagnosing or overpromising.
- Design for distracted, low-energy moments as well as calm ones.
- Keep urgent support visible and easy to understand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, strong contrast, and language that does not assume a diagnosis, identity, or life situation.
